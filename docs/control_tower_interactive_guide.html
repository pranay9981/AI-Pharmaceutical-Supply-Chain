Top Tough Meeting Questions & Bulletproof Answers

Q1: Why shouldn't we just use ChatGPT or an LLM to generate our demand forecasts directly? ▼
The Bulletproof Answer:
"In pharmaceutical manufacturing, an ungrounded hallucination leads directly to antibiotic stockouts or multimillion-dollar lot write-offs. LLMs are non-deterministic next-token predictors; they cannot guarantee convex loss optimization or mathematical consistency over recursive 26-week rollouts. In our architecture, generative AI serves strictly as an evidence-grounded briefing copilot citing database IDs. The numbers themselves are computed by convex, closed-form L2-regularized Ridge regression, achieving 7.76% WAPE with zero stochastic variation."
Q2: How do you mathematically guarantee that your machine learning model didn't cheat via data leakage? ▼
The Bulletproof Answer:
"We enforce strict chronological time-series splitting: 78 weeks of training (2024-10-28 to 2026-04-20), 13 weeks of validation, and 13 weeks of completely unseen holdout testing with zero date overlap ($\Delta t \ge 7\text{ days}$). All 31 engineered features use strictly backward-looking data ($t-1$ or earlier). Furthermore, our automated leakage audit engine (src/forecasting/prep.py) programmatically audits correlations against future targets before training, confirming a correlation of exactly 0.0000."
Q3: How does the system handle sudden pandemic surges or product recall plunges? ▼
The Bulletproof Answer:
"That is precisely what our Day 24 Demand Anomaly Detector (src/forecasting/anomaly.py) accomplishes. It continuously monitors rolling volatility-adjusted Z-scores ($Z \ge 3.0$) and non-parametric Tukey's IQR fences. If demand surges beyond $3\sigma$, it calculates the exact safety stock depletion percentage and days of forward supply consumed, alerting planners with an expedited packaging directive. If demand collapses, it flags inventory aging and excess holding costs, advising planners to throttle batch release."
Q4: Can this AI system place purchase orders or reschedule lines without human knowledge? ▼
The Bulletproof Answer:
"No. By design, our Pydantic schemas enforce human_approval_required = True. Any recommendation generated by the system is structurally blocked from automated execution without authorized planner sign-off. If a script or user attempts to set human_approval_required = False, the Pydantic validator raises an immediate validation exception."
Q5: How does FEFO allocation work when a hospital has strict shelf-life requirements? ▼
The Bulletproof Answer:
"Our FEFO engine (src/engine/inventory.py) checks lot quality release status, regulatory recall flags, and destination market MARSL (Minimum Acceptable Remaining Shelf Life) thresholds from bridge_sku_market. Lots with less than 90 days of shelf life upon anticipated arrival cannot be dispatched to hospital customers, preventing regulatory rejection at the receiving dock."
